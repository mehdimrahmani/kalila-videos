ABOUT KALILA & DIMNA
Kalila & Dimna is a book of animal fables that began as the ancient Indian Panchatantra. It was translated into Middle Persian, then into Arabic by Ibn al-Muqaffa, and later into Persian, becoming one of the most widely translated story collections in the world.

This is an original animated adaptation of a public-domain classic. The script, animation, character voices and santur music were all made for this channel, to share the wisdom of Persian storytelling with children everywhere.
