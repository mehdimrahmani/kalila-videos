- **DIMNA:** Kalila, if a giant snake stole your 3 eggs every spring, and you were just a tiny crow, how would you stop him?
- **KALILA:** Hmm. I would peck him on the nose! Peck, peck, peck!

## 1. HOME — the crows' nest and three eggs
- **NARRATOR:** Once upon a time, two crows built a cozy nest, high up in a tall tree. Soon, there were three little eggs in it.
- **MOTHER CROW:** Look, my dear! Three eggs! Soon we will have three little babies!

## 2. SNAKE — the greedy snake climbs up
- **NARRATOR:** But down at the bottom of the tree, in a dark hole, lived a big, greedy snake.
- **SNAKE:** Hsss... Eggs! I smell eggs! Yummy eggs for my breakfast!
- **NARRATOR:** Every spring, the snake crept up the tree and stole the crows' eggs. And now, here he came again!
- **FATHER CROW:** Go away, snake! Leave our eggs alone!
- **SNAKE:** Hsss! You are too small to stop me. I will come back tomorrow!

## 3. WORRY — too small to fight
- **MOTHER CROW:** Oh no! What can we do? He is so big, and we are so small.
- **FATHER CROW:** Don't cry, my dear. Let's ask our friend, the wise old jackal. He always knows what to do.

## 4. JACKAL — the wise jackal's plan
- **NARRATOR:** So the father crow flew to the wise jackal and told him everything.
- **WISE JACKAL:** Hmm. Don't fight the snake with your little beak. Use your head instead!
- **WISE JACKAL:** Fly to the king's garden. Find something shiny, and make sure the guards see you take it. Then drop it into the snake's hole.
- **FATHER CROW:** Into the snake's hole? Oh! I see! What a clever plan!

## 5. GARDEN — the princess's necklace
- **NARRATOR:** In the king's garden, the princess had left her shiny necklace on a stone, beside the pool.
- **NARRATOR:** Swoosh! Down flew the crow, and he picked it up with his beak.
- **GUARD:** Hey! That crow took the princess's necklace! After him!

## 6. CHASE — follow me, guards!
- **NARRATOR:** The crow flew slowly, slowly, so the guards could follow him, all the way back to the tall tree.
- **FATHER CROW:** This way! Follow me! Not too fast...

## 7. DROP — plop! into the snake's hole
- **NARRATOR:** Then, plop! He dropped the necklace right into the snake's hole.
- **GUARD:** It fell in that hole! Let's get it out with my stick!
- **SNAKE:** Hsss! Who is poking my house?
- **GUARD:** A snake! Shoo! Shoo! Go away, and never come back!
- **SNAKE:** Hsss! Help! All right, all right, I'm going! I'm going!
- **NARRATOR:** And the snake slid away, far, far away, and he never came back. The guards took the necklace home to the princess.

## 8. HATCH — three baby crows
- **NARRATOR:** And soon, crack, crack, crack! Three baby crows came out of their eggs.
- **BABY CROWS:** Caw! Caw! Caw!
- **MOTHER CROW:** Thank you, wise jackal! A clever head is better than a big beak!
