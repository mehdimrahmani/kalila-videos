## HOSTS OPEN (under the tree)
- **DIMNA:** Kalila, you would never open your mouth up in the sky, right? But 1 flying tortoise did, and almost lost everything!
- **KALILA:** A flying tortoise? Tortoises can't fly!
- **DIMNA:** This one did, with two kind ducks, and one little stick. Listen!

## 1. POND — the chatty tortoise
- **NARRATOR:** Once upon a time, by a little green pond, there lived a tortoise and two ducks. They were the best of friends.
- **NARRATOR:** The tortoise was kind, but he loved to talk. He talked all day long!
- **TORTOISE:** Good morning, ducks! Did you see the sun? Did you see that bee? Did you see my new flower? Did you...
- **DUCK:** Yes, yes, dear friend. We saw everything!

## 2. DRY — the pond dries up
- **NARRATOR:** But one summer, the sun was very hot, and no rain came. Day by day, the little pond dried up.
- **LITTLE DUCK:** Oh no! Our pond is almost gone!
- **DUCK:** We must fly to the big blue lake, far away, over the hills.
- **TORTOISE:** Fly? But I cannot fly! Please don't leave me here alone!
- **LITTLE DUCK:** We will never leave you, friend. Let us think.

## 3. IDEA — a stick, and not one word
- **DUCK:** I have an idea! We will hold a stick, one end each. You bite the middle, and hold on tight!
- **LITTLE DUCK:** But remember: do not open your mouth. Not one word, until we land!
- **TORTOISE:** Not one word? Easy! I promise! I will be as quiet as a stone!

## 4. LIFT — up, up, up
- **NARRATOR:** So the tortoise bit the stick. The ducks flapped their wings, and up, up, up they flew!
- **NARRATOR:** The tortoise saw the trees, the hills, and the clouds. Oh, how he wanted to talk! But he kept his mouth shut.

## 5. VILLAGE — what a silly tortoise!
- **NARRATOR:** Then they flew over a little village. The children looked up, and they pointed and laughed.
- **GIRL:** Look! Look! Two ducks are carrying a tortoise!
- **BOY:** Ha ha! What a silly tortoise!
- **NARRATOR:** The tortoise got very cross. Silly? Me? He wanted to answer so much!
- **DUCK:** Mm! Mm-mm!
- **TORTOISE:** I am not silly! I am...

## 6. FALL — flump! into the hay
- **NARRATOR:** Oh no! He opened his mouth, and he let go of the stick! Down, down, down fell the tortoise...
- **NARRATOR:** ...right into a big, soft pile of hay! Flump!
- **LITTLE DUCK:** Friend! Friend! Are you all right?

## 7. HAY — I should have kept my mouth shut
- **TORTOISE:** I'm all right. My shell is strong. But, oh, I should have kept my mouth shut.
- **DUCK:** Next time, friend: think first, then speak!

## 8. LAKE — slowly, slowly, together
- **NARRATOR:** So the three friends walked the rest of the way together, slowly, slowly, all the way to the big blue lake.
- **TORTOISE:** Mm-hmm! Mm-hmm!
- **NARRATOR:** And this time, the tortoise thought very carefully before every word.

- **DIMNA:** Think before you speak! Your words can wait a little.
