# Episode 2 — The Monkey and the Tortoise (بوزینه و باخه)

Moral: A true friend uses their head; trust wisely. Persian word of the day: «دوست» (doost = friend).
Voices: Narrator af_heart 0.92 · Kalila am_michael 0.92 · Dimna am_puck 1.0 · Monkey am_echo 1.02 (pitched up) · Tortoise bm_lewis 0.9 (pitched down) · Mrs. Tortoise bf_emma 0.95.
Child-safe retelling: no one is hurt; the monkey tricks his way home and the tortoise says sorry and learns.

Title card (3 s): KALILA & DIMNA — The Monkey and the Tortoise — Episode 2


## HOSTS OPEN (under the tree)
- **DIMNA:** Kalila, if your best friend asked you to ride on his back across the sea, would you say yes?
- **KALILA:** Of course. A friend is a friend.
- **DIMNA:** Hmm. Even if your friend had a secret plan? Sit down, brother. Let me tell you a story.

## 1. SHORE — the old monkey in the fig tree
- **NARRATOR:** Once upon a time, an old monkey lived all alone in a big fig tree by the sea. He used to be the king of the monkeys, but now he was old, and a younger monkey had taken his place.
- **MONKEY:** Oh well. At least I have my tree, my figs, and the sea breeze.

## 2. MEET — the tortoise swims up
- **NARRATOR:** One sunny day, a tortoise swam up to the shore and rested under the fig tree.
- **MONKEY:** Hello down there! You look tired. Would you like a sweet fig?
- **TORTOISE:** A fig? For me? Oh, thank you, kind monkey!

## 3. FRIENDS — days of figs and laughter
- **NARRATOR:** The tortoise came back the next day, and the next, and the next. The monkey threw down figs, the tortoise told stories of the sea, and they laughed and laughed. They became best friends.

## 4. HOME — the tortoise's island at dusk
- **NARRATOR:** But back home, on a little island, Mrs. Tortoise was not happy at all.
- **MRS. TORTOISE:** Every day you are with that monkey! You never stay home with me anymore.
- **NARRATOR:** So she pretended to be very sick, and she said something terrible.
- **MRS. TORTOISE:** Oh, I feel so ill! The only thing that can make me well... is a monkey's heart!

## 5. WORRY — what shall I do?
- **TORTOISE:** A monkey's heart? But the monkey is my friend... Oh, what shall I do?
- **NARRATOR:** The tortoise thought and thought. And then, sadly, he made a bad choice.

## 6. INVITE — a dinner invitation
- **TORTOISE:** Dear friend, come to my island for a big dinner! Climb on my back, and I will carry you across the sea.
- **MONKEY:** A dinner party? How lovely! Let's go!

## 7. SEA — the ride across the sea
- **NARRATOR:** So the monkey climbed onto the tortoise's back, and off they swam, over the deep blue sea.

## 8. MIDDLE — the truth, and the monkey's clever idea
- **NARRATOR:** But in the middle of the sea, the tortoise stopped.
- **TORTOISE:** Friend, I must tell you the truth. My wife is sick, and she says only a monkey's heart can make her well.
- **MONKEY:** My heart?!
- **NARRATOR:** The monkey was far from home, and he could not swim. But he did not panic. He used his head.
- **MONKEY:** Oh, silly friend, why didn't you tell me before? Monkeys never take their hearts on trips! I left mine hanging in my fig tree. Let's go back and get it!

## 9. BACK — home to the fig tree
- **NARRATOR:** So the tortoise turned around and swam all the way back. The moment they reached the shore, the monkey leapt up into his tree.
- **TORTOISE:** Now, friend, bring your heart, and let's go!
- **MONKEY:** My heart was with me all along! A true friend would never trick me. Go home, tortoise. I will never ride on your back again.
- **TORTOISE:** Oh... I was wrong. I am sorry, my friend.
- **NARRATOR:** And the tortoise swam home, sad, and a little bit wiser. He had lost the best friend he ever had.

## HOSTS CLOSE — moral, Persian word of the day, next story
- **KALILA:** So the monkey stayed calm, and he used his head to get home safe.
- **DIMNA:** And the tortoise learned: never trick a friend! A true friend uses their head, and trusts wisely.
- **DIMNA:** Persian word of the day! In Persian, a friend is doost. Doost!
- **KALILA:** Doost. A friend. Let's always be a good doost.
- **KALILA:** And next time: a ring-dove, her friends, and a hunter's net.
