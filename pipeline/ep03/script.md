- **DIMNA:** Kalila, what if you and five friends were stuck in a hunter's net, and you could never fly home again? What would you do?
- **KALILA:** Hmm. I would pull and pull, as hard as I can!
- **DIMNA:** All by yourself? Ha! Sit down, brother. Today's story will show you a better way.

## 1. MEADOW — the crow sees a hunter spread his net
- **NARRATOR:** Once upon a time, in a green meadow, there stood a tall tree. A crow lived at the top, and he saw everything.
- **NARRATOR:** One morning, the crow saw a hunter. The hunter spread a big net on the grass, and he threw grain all over it.
- **HUNTER:** Hee hee. Now I will hide, and wait for some hungry birds.
- **CROW:** Oh no. That is a trap! I must watch and see what happens.

## 2. FLOCK — the Ring-Dove and her friends fly by
- **NARRATOR:** Soon, a flock of doves came flying by. Their leader was the Ring-Dove. She had a beautiful golden ring around her neck, and she was very wise.
- **YOUNG DOVE:** Look, everyone! Yummy grain! Let's eat!
- **RING-DOVE:** Wait, little ones! Grain in the middle of a meadow? Something is not right...

## 3. CAUGHT — snap!
- **NARRATOR:** But the young doves were too hungry. Down they flew. And snap! The net closed tight around them all.
- **YOUNG DOVE:** Help! I'm stuck! I can't get out!
- **HUNTER:** Ha ha! Look at all those doves! What a lucky day for me!
- **NARRATOR:** The doves flapped and pulled, each one on its own. But the more they pulled, the tighter the net became.

## 4. TOGETHER — the Ring-Dove's idea
- **RING-DOVE:** Stop! Listen to me, friends! If each of us pulls alone, we will never get free. But if we all fly up together, at the very same time, we can lift this net into the sky!
- **RING-DOVE:** Ready? One... two... three... FLY!

## 5. FLY — the net flies away
- **NARRATOR:** And up they went! All together, with the whole net, high into the sky!
- **HUNTER:** Hey! Come back with my net!

## 6. CHASE — the hunter gives up
- **NARRATOR:** The hunter ran after them, over the hills and through the fields. And the curious crow followed too.
- **HUNTER:** Huff, puff... They are too fast for me. Oh well. I will go home and have some soup.

## 7. ROCK — Zirak the mouse
- **RING-DOVE:** Now, friends, let's fly to my old friend Zirak, the mouse. He will help us.
- **NARRATOR:** They landed beside a big rock with a little hole in it.
- **RING-DOVE:** Zirak! Zirak, my friend! Are you home?
- **ZIRAK:** Who's there? Oh, it's you, my dear Ring-Dove! What happened? Don't worry, I will chew this net for you!

## 8. CHEW — free my friends first
- **RING-DOVE:** Thank you, Zirak. But please, free my friends first. Free me last.
- **ZIRAK:** Last? But why?
- **RING-DOVE:** Because I am their leader. If you get tired, I don't want any of them left behind.
- **ZIRAK:** Now I see why your friends love you so much!
- **NARRATOR:** Nibble, nibble, nibble! One by one, Zirak set every dove free. And last of all, the Ring-Dove.

## 9. FRIENDS — the crow joins them
- **CROW:** Wow, I saw everything! You help each other! Can I be your friend too?
- **ZIRAK:** Hmm... A crow? Well, all right! Friends help friends. Welcome!
- **NARRATOR:** And from that day on, the doves, the mouse and the crow were the best of friends.
