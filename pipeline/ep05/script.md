## HOSTS OPEN (under the tree)
- **DIMNA:** Kalila, you would never trust a hungry heron, right? But 10 little fish did, and they almost lost their home!
- **KALILA:** Ten little fish? Oh no! Who saved them?
- **DIMNA:** Someone very small, with two very strong claws. Listen!

## 1. LAKE — the old, slow heron
- **NARRATOR:** Once upon a time, by a sparkling blue lake, there lived an old heron. Every day, he caught fish for his dinner.
- **NARRATOR:** But now he was old and slow, and the fish swam away too fast.
- **HERON:** Oh, my legs are tired, and my beak is slow. How will I get my dinner?
- **HERON:** Hmm... If I cannot catch them, I will trick them!

## 2. TRICK — the heron's sad story
- **NARRATOR:** So the heron stood by the water, looking very, very sad. A little crab saw him from her rock.
- **CRAB:** Uncle Heron, why are you so sad today?
- **HERON:** Oh, little crab! I heard the fishermen talking. Tomorrow they will come with big nets, and take all the fish away!

## 3. FEAR — please help us!
- **FISH:** Oh no! What can we do?
- **LITTLE FISH:** Please help us, Uncle Heron!
- **HERON:** Don't worry, my dears. Behind the big rocks, there is a beautiful new lake. I will carry you there, one by one.
- **NARRATOR:** The fish believed his sweet words.

## 4. CARRY — the tiny muddy puddle
- **NARRATOR:** So every day, the heron carried a fish away in his beak. But there was no beautiful lake. He dropped them into a tiny muddy puddle, behind the rocks.
- **HERON:** Hee hee! My little dinner pond. I will eat you all, one by one!
- **LITTLE FISH:** Help! This is not a beautiful lake!

## 5. ASK — take me too!
- **CRAB:** Uncle Heron, take me too! I want to see the new lake.
- **HERON:** A crab? Hmm, crab for dinner... Yes, yes! Climb up on my neck!

## 6. FLIGHT — the crab looks down
- **NARRATOR:** Up, up they flew, over the big rocks. The crab looked down, and she saw the tiny puddle, full of scared fish!
- **CRAB:** That is not a lake! You tricked us!

## 7. PINCH — let me go!
- **NARRATOR:** So the clever crab held on to the heron's neck with her claws, and... pinch!
- **HERON:** Ow! Ow! Let me go!
- **CRAB:** First, put me down. And never trick the fish again!
- **HERON:** Ow! All right, all right! I'm sorry! I'm going!
- **NARRATOR:** The heron put the crab down, and flew far, far away. And he never came back.

## 8. HOME — the fish swim home
- **NARRATOR:** Then the crab dug a little stream with her claws, from the puddle, all the way back to the lake. And all the fish swam home!
- **FISH:** Hooray! Thank you, clever crab!
- **CRAB:** Remember, friends: sweet words are not always true words!
