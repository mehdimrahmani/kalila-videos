# English for Mehdi — from the basics (برنامه‌ی زبان مهدی)

Goal: in 24 weeks, from "alphabet and a few words" to reading the channel's titles, captions and Rumi
translations, writing short messages, and understanding slow, clear speech (about CEFR A2).
Rhythm: 15 minutes a day, Saturday–Thursday a new lesson; Friday a short review + weekly quiz.
A new lesson is ready every morning at ~06:15 (Tehran) in a new conversation. Explanations in Persian,
examples in English with pronunciation written in Persian letters, plus a short audio file.

## Every lesson (15 min)
1. Review (3 min): 5 words from earlier lessons (spaced repetition: words he got wrong come back sooner).
2. New words (4 min): 5 words, each with Persian meaning, pronunciation in Persian letters, one example.
3. One small grammar point (3 min): one idea only, 2-3 examples.
4. Read (2 min): 2-3 short sentences — from our own channels whenever possible (titles, Rumi lines in English).
5. Practice (3 min): 5 short questions + write ONE sentence of his own. He replies in the same conversation;
   the teacher corrects kindly and records the result.

## Phases
### Phase 1 · Weeks 1–4 · Foundations (پایه)
- Letters → sounds (phonics), short/long vowels, how English spelling maps to sound; the 20 most common sight words
- Greetings, introducing yourself; pronouns (I, you, he, she, we, they); to be (am / is / are)
- Numbers 0–100, days, months, time; this / that / these / those; a / an / the; plurals
- Family, home, colours, everyday objects; simple questions with "be" (Is it…? Are you…?)
### Phase 2 · Weeks 5–10 · Everyday life (زندگی روزمره)
- Present simple (I work / he works), daily routine, frequency words (always, usually, never)
- have / has, can / can't, there is / there are, prepositions of place and time (in, on, at, under…)
- Question words (what, where, when, who, why, how), food, city, transport, weather, feelings
### Phase 3 · Weeks 11–16 · Work and stories (کار و داستان)
- Past simple (regular and the 40 most common irregular verbs), telling a short story
- Present continuous (I am working), future (going to / will)
- Work English for his job: meeting, report, budget, project, department, deadline, schedule, approve
- Reading our own content: YouTube titles, podcast descriptions, Instagram captions
### Phase 4 · Weeks 17–24 · Reading Rumi and writing (خواندن مولانا و نوشتن)
- Comparatives and superlatives, should / must / have to, present perfect (have you ever…?)
- Reading the channel's Rumi translations line by line (he already knows the Persian — perfect for meaning)
- Writing: short messages, a simple email, a 3-sentence description of our channel
- Listening: slow audio of our own lines, then natural speed

## Rules for the teacher (each daily session)
- Persian explanations, short and warm; never more than 5 new words a day; never overload.
- Pronunciation in Persian letters for every new word, e.g. guest = «گِست».
- If he did not answer yesterday's practice, do NOT move on: today = short review of yesterday + 2 new words only.
- Record progress in english/progress.json and push (lesson number, words learned, words to repeat, notes).
- Every Friday: weekly review + a 10-question quiz; every 4 weeks: a short check of the whole phase.
